Stage 3 of 7

Case Management Conference 1

What happens

  • A Registrar sits down (virtually or in person) with you and the respondent to review the claim.
  • They'll check whether either side is open to mediation, to try to settle things without a full trial.
  • If mediation isn't happening, the Registrar sets a deadline for both sides to prepare and exchange their documents.
  • This is usually short and administrative — not where your case gets decided.
Cost None
Rough duration ~1 month
