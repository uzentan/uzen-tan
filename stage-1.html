Stage 1 of 7

File with TADM

What happens

  • Once your notice period has ended, you file a claim with TADM (the Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management).
  • TADM assigns you a claims officer, who tries to help you and your employer settle things informally.
  • If that doesn't resolve it, TADM issues a certificate — this is what lets you bring your case to the Tribunal.
  • No certificate, no Tribunal case — so this step isn't optional, even if mediation goes nowhere.
Cost ~$30–$60
Rough duration ~1 month
