Stage 4 of 7

Paperwork & evidence

What happens

  • You prepare a Witness Statement (your written account of events) and a Hearing Bundle (all your evidence, organized and indexed).
  • Got audio or video evidence? It has to be transcribed by an approved transcription company first — you can't just hand over the raw recording.
  • The recordings and transcripts then get burned onto 3 DVDs: one for the Tribunal, one for the respondent, one to keep for yourself.
  • Everything is submitted physically, plus a copy sent by mail — this stage is more admin-heavy than any other.
Cost Varies (transcription)
Rough duration ~1 month
