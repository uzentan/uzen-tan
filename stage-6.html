Trial

How the day runs

  • It's a full day. You represent yourself — no lawyers are allowed in the room for either side.
  • You're sworn in, then both sides give their opening statements.
  • You're cross-examined — first by the other side, then by the judge.
  • There's a lunch break partway through.
  • The respondent's witness testifies and is cross-examined too.
  • You get a short window to prepare a closing statement before a verdict date is set.
Length Full day
Format Zoom or in person
