Stage 2 of 7

File with the ECT via CJTS

What happens

  • With your TADM certificate in hand, you file your formal claim with the Employment Claims Tribunal (ECT) through CJTS — the Community Justice and Tribunals System online portal.
  • You'll receive a "claim summary" document that lays out exactly what you're claiming.
  • It's on you to serve this on the respondent (your employer) yourself — by email, post, or in person.
  • Hold onto proof you served it. The Tribunal may ask you to show it later.
Cost ~$30
Rough duration ~1 month
