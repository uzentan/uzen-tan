This is a plain-English walkthrough of what actually happens during an Employment Claims Tribunal (ECT) case — written by someone explaining the process, not a lawyer. It isn't legal advice, and it won't answer questions about your specific case. For that, speak to a lawyer or the Community Justice Tribunals System (CJTS).

So you've got an employment dispute. Here's what actually happens.

No jargon, no walls of text — just the seven stages you'll go through, in order, with the costs and rough timelines for each.

Join our community
Heads up: this takes time. Each stage below tends to take roughly a month. Start to finish, most claims take a minimum of about 5 months — often longer. Planning for that up front makes the wait a lot less stressful.

The 7 stages, in order

  2. Stage 2 File with the ECT via CJTS

  4. Stage 4 Paperwork & evidence
  5. Stage 5 Case Management Conference 2
