Stage 7 of 7

Verdict

What happens

  • The Tribunal issues its written decision roughly a month after the trial.
  • The verdict states who won and, if you were awarded anything, how much.
  • If you disagree with the outcome, there are limited grounds to appeal — this is worth raising with a lawyer.
Cost None
Rough duration ~1 month after trial
